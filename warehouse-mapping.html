Frequently Asked Questions

Which CAD drawing formats does GREXON support?

GREXON directly ingests AutoCAD DWG, standard DXF (Drawing Exchange Format), and vector architectural SVGs, converting vector polylines into topologically valid warehouse geometry.

Are GREXON digital maps measurable to real physical dimensions?

Yes. GREXON extracts real-world scale factors from CAD drawings, maintaining millimeter-accurate measurements across aisle widths, column clearances, racking depths, and transit lanes.
