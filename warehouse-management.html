Frequently Asked Questions

How does GREXON organize warehouse racks, bays, and storage bins?

GREXON structures warehouse storage into a strict spatial hierarchy: Facility, Zone, Aisle, Rack, Bay, Shelf, and Storage Bin. Each bin is assigned specific physical dimensions and metric XYZ coordinates.

What is dynamic slotting optimization in GREXON?

Dynamic slotting evaluates product velocity and automatically recommends relocation of high-velocity items to accessible lower-tier bays near packing docks, cutting order picking travel times.
