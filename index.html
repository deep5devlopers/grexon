Frequently Asked Questions

Verifiable answers regarding GREXON's warehouse management and spatial navigation architecture.

What is GREXON warehouse management software?

GREXON is an enterprise spatial intelligence and warehouse operations platform that unifies digital CAD mapping, physical storage bin tracking, and obstacle-aware indoor navigation into a single connected operational environment.

How does GREXON convert warehouse drawings into digital maps?

GREXON ingests industry-standard vector CAD formats (DXF, DWG) and automatically extracts architectural wall boundaries, structural columns, safety perimeters, and racking arrangements to create measurable 2D and 3D digital twins with real spatial geometry.

How does GREXON indoor navigation calculate pick paths?

GREXON utilizes a weighted Voronoi and navmesh pathfinding engine running customized A* and Dijkstra algorithms. It accounts for turn radiuses, dynamic obstacle exclusions, and traveling salesperson problem (TSP) order sequencing to minimize picker walking distances.

Does GREXON integrate with existing ERP and WMS solutions?

Yes. GREXON pairs directly with systems like SAP EWM, Oracle NetSuite, Blue Yonder, and Manhattan Associates using bi-directional REST APIs and high-frequency WebSocket streams, ensuring inventory levels and physical coordinates stay in continuous synchronization.
